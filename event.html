Russell and Maria's Beach Wedding

Date: October 3rd, 2026
5:30 PM - 10:00 PM

Location: 2001 S Ocean Blvd, Myrtle Beach, SC 29577

Category: Wedding

Celebrate Russell and Maria's wedding with an evening of love, laughter, and memories shared with family and friends and fellow classmates. All UNCG Computer Science majors are invited to attend

Wedding Day

Join Russell and Maria as they exchange their vows on the beach, followed by an evening of celebration with family and friends at Bluewater Resort.

Ceremony

5:30 PM–6:00 PM

We request the pleasure of your company as we exchange our vows on the beach at half past five in the afternoon.

Reception

6:00 PM–10:00 PM

The celebration continues at six o'clock in the Resort Salon on the third floor of Bluewater Resort.

Schedule

  • 5:30 PM | Beach ceremony begins
  • 6:00 PM | Reception begins in the Resort Salon
  • 10:00 PM | Celebration concludes

What to Expect

The ceremony takes place outdoors on the beach, and the reception takes place indoors on the resort's third floor. Please plan your footwear with the sandy ceremony location in mind. Semi-formal, so dress nice, but appropiately for the beach. Be respectful to all hotel guests, as we are not the only people using the beach

Participation

We will be giving out complimentary bubbles bottles so that you can blow bubbles during the exit of the ceremony. We will also have a qr code posted that will take you to a app where you will have 25 pictures to take that will be uploaded online as part of our wedding photos.
