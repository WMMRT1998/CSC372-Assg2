About Us

Welcome to the Campus Event Guide! We provide information about upcoming events on campus, or in the bigger Greensboro area. We are ran by the effort of individual students. Our goal is to help students connect with one another, and have great experiences while seeking their educational aspirations.

Benefits That We Provide

  • Connecting you to other students with similar interests
  • Giving you opportunities to participate in events you may not know about
  • Extracurricular educational opportunities
